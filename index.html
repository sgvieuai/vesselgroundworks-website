Water-wise rebates

Utah may pay you to replace your lawn

Utah County homeowners can get paid to replace lawn with water-wise landscaping through Central Utah Water's Landscape Incentive Program. The rate depends on your town.

Two rules matter most. The program has to visit and approve the project before any grass comes out, and half the new area has to be planted, so a rock-only yard won't qualify. In towns with secondary water, those site visits pause from October 15 to April 15. Winter is the time to plan: we'll measure, photograph and draw the plan you need, so you can apply first thing in spring.
