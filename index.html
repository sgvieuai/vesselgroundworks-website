Questions

Common questions

What services does Vessel Landscape offer?

Xeriscaping and low-water landscaping, yard renovations, boulder and block retaining walls, concrete curbing, full yard installs for new builds, sprinkler installation and repair, and the excavation, grading and concrete removal underneath it all.

Do you take small jobs as well as big projects?

Yes. We handle everything from a single broken sprinkler head to full yard installs on new builds and multi-phase excavation projects.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Springville and work across Utah County, including Mapleton, Spanish Fork, Salem, Payson, Provo and Orem. Larger projects can go anywhere along the Wasatch Front. Outside that? Text us and ask.

Do you specialize in water-efficient landscaping?

Yes. Rock work, drought-tolerant planting, drip irrigation, grading and edging are a big part of what we do, and we can explain how Utah's lawn-replacement rebate works.

Can you do the groundwork before landscaping or concrete?

Yes. We do the excavation, grading, trenching, drainage and prep so the finished landscape or concrete starts on a solid, level base.

Are estimates free?

Yes. Call or text and we'll set up a time to look at the job. You get a clear price before any work starts.
