How much does xeriscaping cost in Utah County?

Most full xeriscape conversions in Utah run about $5 to $15 per square foot, so roughly $5,000 to $15,000 for a 1,000 sq ft lawn. Utah County homeowners can get $1.50 to $3.00 per square foot back through the state's lawn-replacement rebate.

What moves the price

Why one yard costs more than another

How much lawn you keep

Keeping a small lawn where the kids play costs less than converting every square foot, and it can still qualify for the rebate on the part you replace.

Plants vs. rock

Rock covers ground for less than a planted bed, but the rebate needs half the new area planted, so most yards land on a mix.

Curbing, boulders & edging

Concrete curbing, boulders and steel edging make a yard look finished and add to the cost.

Dirt work & access

Steep grades, poor drainage or a tight side gate mean more excavation time. We do this work ourselves, so there's no second contractor to pay.

Your sprinkler system

Every job caps spray zones and runs drip to the new plants. A system already in good shape keeps that part of the cost down.

Size

Bigger areas cost more in total but usually less per square foot, since setup and equipment are spread over more ground.

Questions

Common questions

How much does xeriscaping cost per square foot in Utah?

Most full conversions run about $5 to $15 per square foot. A simple yard with rock and light planting sits at the low end. Curbing, boulders, dense planting and big grade changes push it higher.

How much does it cost to replace a 1,000 sq ft lawn?

Typically $5,000 to $15,000 before rebates. In Spanish Fork, Salem and Payson the lawn-replacement rebate is $3.00 per sq ft, or $3,000. In Springville, Mapleton, Provo and Orem it's $1.50 per sq ft, or $1,500.

Does the rebate pay for the whole project?

No. It covers part of the cost. The program also has rules: it has to approve the project before any grass comes out, half the new area has to be planted, and artificial turf, concrete and pavers don't qualify.

Does a backyard cost more than a front yard?

Mostly it comes down to access. If our equipment fits through the gate, a backyard prices about the same as a front yard of the same size. A tight gate or steep side yard means more hand work and more time.

How much does it cost to xeriscape a park strip?

Park strips are small, so setup and hauling make up a bigger share of the job, and the price per square foot usually lands toward the high end. The lawn-replacement rebate covers park strips too. See park strip landscaping.

Is rock cheaper than sod?

Up front they can cost about the same, depending on the rock and the yard. The difference is what comes after: rock and drip-watered plants don't need mowing and use far less water every summer.

Are estimates free?

Yes. Call or text and we'll come look at the yard. You get a clear price before any work starts.

When should I start planning?

Winter. In towns with secondary water, the rebate program's site visits pause from October 15 to April 15. If your plan and measurements are ready, you can apply as soon as visits start again in spring.
